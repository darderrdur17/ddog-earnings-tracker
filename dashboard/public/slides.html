02 · Why Datadog

Usage- and subscription-based revenue, tied to cloud workloads

  • FY 2025 revenue $3.43bn (+28%). Majority-annual subscriptions.
  • Committed usage recognized ratably or as used; monthly usage and overage on top.
  • 90% of ARR from customers with ARR ≥ $100k; NRR ~120% from existing-customer usage.
  • RPO $3.46bn at year-end 2025. Sells through AWS / Azure / GCP.

NRR, RPO, and large-customer counts are 10-K disclosures — not a quarterly Company Facts series we nowcast.

04 · Method

Simple model, honest baselines

  • Target: SEC revenue YoY. Q4 = FY − (Q1+Q2+Q3) when needed.
  • Sample: 14 quarters, 2023Q1–2026Q2.
  • Validated call: ridge on lag-1 npm only. No same-quarter leak.
  • Intra-quarter stub: same calendar-day npm YoY through 1 Sep 2026 (63/92 days) — coincident, not a lead.
  • Baselines: persistence + company 8-K. No Street scrape. No future row in training.

09 · Limits

Small sample, honest caveats

  • Fourteen quarters only — that is why persistence is the baseline.
  • npm includes CI and mirrors; it is not billable usage.
  • Q4 revenue is sometimes FY residual.
  • Wiki jumps on news; it is not in the call.
  • No Street vendor — company 8-K only.
